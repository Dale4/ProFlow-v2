# Scheduling

Scheduling assigns work orders to crews and time slots without double-booking people or overloading a day.

## What it covers

- Crew availability
- Job duration / travel
- Calendar or board view
- Conflicts and reschedule

## Constraints (draft)

- One crew cannot be in two places at once
- Measure and install may be separate visits
- Warehouse readiness may block an install slot
