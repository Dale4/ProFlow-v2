# Work orders

A work order is the job record that ties a customer, site, products (windows/doors), measurements, and crew work together.

## What it covers

- Customer / site
- Line items (windows, doors, sizes)
- Status through the job lifecycle
- Notes, attachments, and handoff between roles

## Status (draft — confirm against the data model)

- Draft
- Measured
- Scheduled
- In progress
- Complete
- Cancelled / on hold

## Open questions

- Is a work order 1:1 with a job, or can one job have multiple work orders?
- Who can change status at each step?
